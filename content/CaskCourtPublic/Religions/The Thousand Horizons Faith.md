**Primary Region:** Thalmyran Expanse  
**Type:** Mystical polytheism + cosmic cycle belief  
**Theme:** Balance, inevitability, long cycles of rise/fall

### Core Belief

Civilizations rise and fall like tides. Nothing is permanent and thus it is sacred.

### Divine Structure

Not fixed gods, more like **Great Cosmic Forces**:

- The First Flame (creation)
    
- The Deep Silence (ending)
    
- The Turning Tide (change)
    

### Clergy Role
 
- Astronomers
    
- Dream interpreters
    
- Storm ritualists

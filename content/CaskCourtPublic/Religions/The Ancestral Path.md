**Primary Region:** Varkesh Empire  
**Type:** Ancestor + war spirit reverence  
**Theme:** Honor, legacy, strength through memory

### Core Belief

The dead watch the living. Dishonor weakens the living and shames the dead.

### Main Deity: **Hrothus, the Everwarden**

God of:

- Honor
    
- Responsibility
    
- Spirits
### Divine Figures

Not “gods” — but **Ascended Ancestors** and **Primal War Spirits**

Most revered:

- The First Shield (defense of the people)
    
- The Winter Mother (survival, endurance)
    
- The River Father (guidance through hardship)
    

### Clergy Role

- Battlefield rites
    
- Genealogy keepers
    
- Oath witnesses
    
- Spirit interpreters

(Large, proud, disciplined, slightly more martial)

A colder, highland and river-fed realm with strong noble houses and elite military traditions.
Often referred to as "The Imperium"

- More rigid hierarchy.
    
- Honor-bound warrior culture.
    
- Strong navy or river fleet.
    
- Suspicious of Aurentine economic dominance.
    

They see themselves as guardians of tradition.

Flavor: Stone fortresses, ironwood forests, wolf insignias, ancestral halls.

Tension: They believe Aurentine influence is slowly strangling their sovereignty.**

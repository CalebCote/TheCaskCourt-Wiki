**Primary Region:** Free Marches of Eldovar  
**Type:** Folk faith / decentralized worship  
**Theme:** Community, survival, local spirits

### No Main Deity, many minor gods
### Core Belief

Divinity lives in:

- Hearth fires
    
- River crossings
    
- Harvest cycles
    
- Town founders
    

### Divine Figures

Thousands of local saints, spirits, and guardian figures.

### Clergy Role

- Festival leaders
    
- Midwives
    
- Traveling shrine keepers
    
- Local historians

**Primary Region:** Aurentine Concord  
**Type:** Structured state religion  
**Theme:** Order, civilization, enlightenment through structure
### Core Belief

The universe trends toward harmony when intelligent beings choose cooperation over chaos.

### Main Deity: **Solvaris, The Golden Arbiter**

God of:

- Law
    
- Civilization
    
- Contracts
    
- Measured progress
    

Depicted as:  
A radiant figure with scales in one hand and a sealed scroll in the other.

### Clergy Role

- Legal witnesses
    
- Treaty blessers
    
- Advisors to nobility
    
- University patrons
